Privacy Policy — Hatchly for Android

Last updated: 25 November 2026. This policy covers the Android game Hatchly : Œufs & Créatures (package com.loukamorici.eclosion) on Google Play. Publisher: Louka Morici, independent developer, Belgium. Contact: zikolouka@gmail.com.

1. Game data stays on your phone

Your progress, gold, creatures and settings are stored only on your device. There is no account and no password. Deleting the app deletes this data.

2. Anonymous sign-in (Google Firebase)

To let players find each other, the app signs in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). This creates a random identifier that is not linked to your name, e-mail or Google account. Firebase Cloud Firestore is used only as a meeting point: it briefly stores a random player identifier, a timestamp and the technical connection data (WebRTC session descriptions, which contain your device's IP address) needed to open a direct connection. Connection messages are deleted as soon as they are read; directory entries expire after a few minutes of inactivity. The "World tree" records and negotiation messages you choose to send are stored in Firestore with your chosen nickname. Firebase is processed by Google: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3. Player-to-player network (Market and Lounge)

The Market and the Lounge work peer-to-peer: phones connect directly to each other (WebRTC, using Google's public STUN servers). What you publish there — your nickname, your listings, prices, purchase requests and Lounge messages (emotes and short texts) — is shared with other players and may be kept in their local cache (up to 500 listings and 100 messages). Like any direct connection, the other phones can see your IP address. Each phone also creates its own anonymous cryptographic key to sign what it sends; it contains no personal data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect the advertising ID, an approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud: how Google uses information from apps. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form is shown before personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

5. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging receives a device token to deliver game notifications.

6. Your rights and deletion

Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction or deletion, and withdraw consent at any time. Uninstalling the app removes local data; anonymous directory entries expire on their own. For any request, including deleting World tree or negotiation entries, write to zikolouka@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

7. Children

Hatchly is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
